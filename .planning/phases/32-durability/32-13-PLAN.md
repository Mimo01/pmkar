<objective>
Close review warning WR-06: the confirm-time recheck must not treat the pre-fetch list as authoritative while a fetch is running.

Purpose: `handleFetch` sets `fetchStatus = 'loading'` but leaves `lastCompleteFetchSignature` unchanged. A confirm that lands mid-fetch therefore removes a member that the in-flight fetch is about to bring back. This weakens T-32-23 ("a member that came back is never removed") and the GDUR-04 "flagged only when actually true" guarantee. All three removal paths go through `readMissingKeysNow` and then `readHintsEligibleNow`, so one guard there covers them all.

Hook decision (the orchestrator asked to decide consistently): leave `useHintsEligible` unchanged. Gating the hook on loading would hide and re-show the whole missing block on every background poll-triggered fetch. It would also unmount any open confirm popover mid-interaction. The display still reflects the last complete fetch, which is truthful. The removal decision is the only place where stale data causes harm, and it uses `readHintsEligibleNow`. A confirm during loading aborts and removes nothing; the popover closes. The user can confirm again after the fetch settles. Silent-abort feedback is IN-06, which is deferred and not added here.

Output: the loading guard, a doc comment recording the hook decision, and vitest coverage.
</objective>
