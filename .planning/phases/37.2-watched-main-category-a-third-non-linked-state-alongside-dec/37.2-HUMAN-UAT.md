## Tests

### 1. Watched nav tab and count badge
steps:
1. Open the app with several Watched tickets, in English, then switch to Slovak.
2. Move a ticket into and out of Watched and watch the nav.
3. Navigate to the Watched tab with Narrator, then NVDA.
expected: The tab reads "Watched" (EN) and "Sledované" (SK). The count badge matches the number of rows on the Watched page and updates on every transition. Narrator and NVDA read the count with the tab name. The Dismissed and Linked tabs show no badge.
result: [pending]

### 2. Linked -> Watch in page, panel and group view
steps:
1. Open a Linked ticket on the detail page. Tab to Watch and activate it.
2. Press Esc, then activate Watch again and press Cancel.
3. Activate Watch again and Confirm.
4. Repeat in the panel and in the group view.
expected: A dialog appears with Cancel focused; nothing moves until Confirm. Esc closes only the dialog (the panel stays open, the page does not navigate back) and focus returns to Watch. After Confirm the ticket is Watched and focus lands on "Restore link to KEY".
result: [pending]

### 3. Dismissed -> Watch is instant with Undo
steps:
1. Open a Dismissed ticket and activate Watch.
2. Reach the Undo toast by keyboard (Tab / F6) and activate Undo.
expected: No dialog. The ticket moves to Watched at once, focus lands on the primary button, and the Undo toast is reachable by keyboard. Undo returns the ticket to Dismissed.
result: [pending]

### 4. Watched -> Deny is instant with Undo
steps:
1. Open a Watched ticket with a remembered key and activate Deny. Press Undo.
2. Repeat with a never-linked Watched ticket.
expected: No dialog. The ticket moves to Dismissed and focus lands on the primary button. Undo returns it to Watched, for both the linked and the never-linked ticket.
result: [pending]

### 5. Watched -> Restore link to KEY
steps:
1. On a Watched ticket with a remembered key, activate "Restore link to KEY" in page and panel. Confirm.
2. Check Jira Cloud afterwards.
expected: A confirmation dialog appears with Cancel focused. After Confirm the ticket is Linked to the same KEY and focus lands on the Linked badge. No new Jira issue is created.
result: [pending]

### 6. Watched never-linked -> Link
steps:
1. On a Watched ticket that was never linked, activate Link (Copy).
expected: The copy preview opens without a confirmation dialog.
result: [pending]

### 7. Watched -> Restore to seen
steps:
1. On the Watched detail, activate Restore. Repeat from the Watched page row Restore.
expected: The ticket returns to New as seen and focus lands on Dismiss (detail) or on a connected element (page), never lost to the document body.
result: [pending]

### 8. Live-region announcements
steps:
1. With Narrator, then NVDA, run every transition: Linked -> Watched, Dismissed -> Watched, Watched -> Dismissed, Watched -> Linked, Watched -> seen, and Undo for each.
expected: Each transition is announced once ("KEY moved to Watched.", "KEY moved to Dismissed.", "KEY linked to ...", "KEY restored."). Nothing is announced on Cancel.
result: [pending]

### 9. Slovak wording
steps:
1. Switch to Slovak. Open the Watch dialog, the Watched page, the empty state, the info card and the toasts.
expected: Texts have correct diacritics and use "tiket" (never "úloha"). The nav reads "Sledované", counts use "Sledovaných" where applicable. Text fits the 400px dialog in EN and SK.
result: [pending]

### 10. App restart keeps Watched tickets
steps:
1. Watch several tickets (with and without remembered keys). Quit and restart the app.
2. Start the app on an existing database created before this phase.
expected: Watched tickets stay Watched with their keys intact after restart. The migration on the pre-existing database succeeds with no data loss and no error.
result: [pending]
