## Tests

### 1. Deny confirmation in page, panel and group view
steps:
1. Open a Linked ticket on the detail page. Activate Deny.
2. Repeat in the panel and in the group view.
3. Press Confirm in each place.
expected: A dialog appears with Cancel focused; nothing moves until Confirm. After Confirm, focus lands on "Restore link to KEY" and the Undo toast is visible and reachable (Tab / F6 into the toast region).
result: [pending]

### 2. Restore link confirmation (remembered key) in page and panel
steps:
1. On a denied ticket with a remembered link, activate Restore link, in page and panel. Confirm.
2. On a never-linked ticket, activate Copy.
expected: The dialog appears for Restore link; after Confirm focus lands on the Linked badge. Never-linked Copy opens the copy preview without a dialog.
result: [pending]

### 3. Esc and Cancel
steps:
1. Open the Deny dialog, press Esc. Repeat with Cancel.
2. Do this on the page and in the panel.
3. Click outside the dialog.
expected: Dialog closes, ticket unchanged, focus returns to the trigger. In the panel Esc does not close the panel; on the page Esc does not navigate back. Outside click does not close the dialog.
result: [pending]

### 4. Narrator and NVDA
steps:
1. With Narrator, then NVDA, open the Deny and Restore link dialogs.
2. Confirm, then separately Cancel.
expected: On open the dialog is announced as an alert dialog with title and description. Result announcements after Confirm ("moved to Dismissed", "linked to") are read once. Nothing is announced on Cancel.
result: [pending]

### 5. Undo after Confirm
steps:
1. Deny a previously Linked ticket, Confirm, press Undo.
2. Restore a denied ticket (lands in New), Dismiss it, press Undo.
expected: First Undo returns the ticket to Linked with the same Cloud key. Second Undo returns it to New, never to Linked.
result: [pending]

### 6. Slovak wording
steps:
1. Switch to Slovak and open both dialogs.
expected: Texts have correct diacritics and say "tiket" (never "úloha"). Deny title reads "Presunúť KEY do Zamietnuté?", link body reads "... do prepojených". Text fits the 400px dialog in EN and SK.
result: [pending]

### 7. Dark theme visual
steps:
1. Switch to dark theme and open both dialogs.
expected: Dialog surface, border, Confirm button (brand, not red) and focus rings are visible.
result: [pending]
