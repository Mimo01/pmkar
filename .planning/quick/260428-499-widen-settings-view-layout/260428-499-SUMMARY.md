# Quick Task 260428-499: Widen Settings View Layout — Summary

## What Changed

**`src/features/connections/SettingsPage.tsx:1225`**
- `max-w-[560px]` → `max-w-[760px]`

All settings sections (source, destination, field mapping, polling, etc.) now render within a 760px max-width container instead of 560px — 200px wider.

## Why This Works

The Tauri window is 1024px. After the 160px sidebar and 64px of horizontal padding, the usable content area is ~800px. The previous 560px cap was leaving ~240px unused. The new 760px cap fills most of that space, giving the 4-column field mapping grid (35fr/35fr/20fr/10fr) substantially more room per column.
